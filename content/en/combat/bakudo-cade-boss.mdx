## How the Fight Works: Phases and Punish Windows

Cade's encounter reads as three escalating phases. Exact percentages and timings may shift with patches, so use the structure below as a framework rather than a script — the *behavior* is what you're memorizing.

| Phase | Cade's Behavior | Your Job | Punish Window |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening | Measured pokes, wide spacing, tests your patience | Hold mid-range, bait the first commit | Short — one hit, then reset |
| Mid | Faster chains, mix-ups between rush and ranged pressure | Dodge late, punish the recovery only | Medium — two to three hits |
| Late | Aggressive pursuit, fewer safe gaps, punish-heavy finishers | Play for survival first, damage second | Tight — take one hit and leave |

### Phase 1: Learn the Telegraphs

The opening phase exists to teach you. Cade's wind-ups are deliberately long here, so spend the first 20 seconds doing almost nothing but watching. Note which animations end with a recovery pose and which ones chain into a second swing. That single observation is worth more than any combo you could practice in a training mode.

### Phase 2: Trade on Your Terms

Once the mix-ups start, stop reacting to every movement. Pick **two** patterns you recognize and commit to punishing only those. A clean two-punish cycle per minute beats a messy five-punish attempt that costs you half your health bar.

### Phase 3: Survive, Then Convert

Late-phase Cade punishes hesitation more than aggression. Reduce your combo length, keep your stamina above a buffer, and let the boss come to you. Player experience suggests most failed runs end in this phase — not because damage is too low, but because the player runs out of defensive options mid-chain.

## Setup Priorities Before You Step In

You can't grind gear in a demo, but you can control how you spend your attention. Here's the priority order that matters most for the BAKUDO Cade boss.

| Priority | Why It Matters | What to Do |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Dodge timing | Panic rolls are the top cause of death | Practice late dodges for two minutes before each run |
| 2. Stamina buffer | Empty stamina = free damage for Cade | Never attack below roughly a quarter bar |
| 3. Ball control | Keeps pressure up during gaps | Use ball actions to reset spacing, not to chase damage |
| 4. Combo restraint | Long strings get you counter-hit | Cap punishes at two or three hits |
| 5. Arena awareness | Corners remove your escape routes | Fight near center, rotate after every exchange |

### Fight Near the Middle

Walls kill runs. When you're pinned, your dodge direction is predictable and Cade's pursuit patterns land far more often. After each exchange, deliberately walk two steps back toward center. It feels slow; it wins fights.

## Five Mistakes That End BAKUDO Cade Boss Runs

These are the recurring failure points in community discussion of the demo. Fix one at a time — trying to correct all five at once usually makes your next run worse.

| Mistake | Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|---|

### The Two-Attempt Rule

Give yourself two focused attempts, then stop and review. Ask one question: *which attack ended the run?* Naming the specific pattern turns a vague "I keep dying" into a solvable problem. Players who do this consistently report faster clears than players who grind attempts back-to-back.

## Reading Cade's Tells Like a Veteran

Pattern recognition is the real skill ceiling here, and it transfers to every other encounter in BAKUDO. Three habits separate clean runs from messy ones.

- **Watch the feet, not the weapon.** Movement tells you the distance of an attack before the animation sells it.
- **Count the rhythm.** Cade's chains usually follow a beat. Clap it out once and the timing sticks.
- **Note the reset.** Every boss has an animation that means "back to neutral." Punish only after you see it.

Once you can predict the reset, the BAKUDO Cade boss stops feeling reactive and starts feeling like a puzzle you've already solved. That's the moment the fight becomes fun instead of frustrating — and it's usually the moment players clear it.

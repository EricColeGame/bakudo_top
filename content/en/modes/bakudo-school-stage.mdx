| What to Watch | The Signal | Your Response |
|---|---|---|
| Opening sequence | How the boss spends the first few seconds | Identify their preferred range and deny it |
| Throw rhythm | The gap between their releases | Slip into the gap and counter |
| Recovery animation | What they do after a big throw | That is your punish window |

## Tips for Your First BAKUDO Demo Run

Because the demo is a boss rush, your first attempt is data collection, not a verdict. A few habits will speed up the learning curve considerably.

- **Watch a full boss pattern before committing to damage.** The game is built around observing movement and finding openings, so your first 30 seconds should be spent reading, not attacking.
- **Treat the ball as a spacing tool first.** Throwing from the right distance creates safer recovery windows than firing at point-blank range.
- **Stop mashing after a hit confirms.** Souls-like and Difficult tags usually mean greedy follow-ups get punished harder than missed openers.
- **Replay the same boss instead of moving on.** Pattern recognition compounds; a fight that felt unfair on attempt one often feels readable by attempt four.
- **Use a controller if you have one.** Xbox and PlayStation pads are both listed as supported, and the demo's real-time combat suits analog input.

| Habit | Common Mistake | Better Approach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening engagement | Charging in immediately | Circle and observe for one full pattern |
| Damage windows | Spamming throws | Two or three committed throws, then reset |
| Losing a fight | Blaming balance | Note the move that killed you, then plan around it |
| Session length | One long grind | Short repeated attempts with a break between |
| Input | Keyboard if uncomfortable | Switch to a supported controller |

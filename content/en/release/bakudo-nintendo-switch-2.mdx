## What Is BAKUDO? Story, Setting, and Characters

BAKUDO is set at Soliris Academy, a prestigious school where students chase the title of top Corestriker. Yuria arrives while following a clue about her missing father. Her plans change fast: during her entrance exam, she breaks the rules to help another examinee in danger, and her admission is revoked. Aria, the strongest student and the top-ranked member of the Stellar Eight, then gives her a brutal ultimatum: beat all eight ranked students within ten days or leave for good.

With Evie as her partner, Yuria takes on the challenge. The academy's polished reputation hides a secret that could affect the fate of the world.

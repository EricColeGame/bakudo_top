## Characters, Setting, and the Soliris Academy Story

The premise is more narrative-driven than the "sports game" label implies. Soliris Academy is the elite school where anyone chasing the title of top Corestriker goes to prove themselves. Yuria enrolls while following the one clue her missing father left behind — but during her entrance exam she breaks the rules to save another examinee, and her admission is revoked on the spot.

That is when Aria, the strongest student in the academy and the top of the Stellar Eight, offers a deal: defeat all eight ranked students within ten days, or leave for good. Yuria takes it, with her partner Evie at her side, and the story escalates from school rivalry into a secret that could decide the fate of the world.
